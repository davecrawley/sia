# ADR 0001: Observer-effect benchmark contract

- Status: Proposed
- Specification revision: 4479bcc19db72f6ad243a87e4b7271496d60d0b7
- Requirements: R01, R12, R38, R40
- Baseline artifact: `benchmarks/observer-effect-baseline.yaml`

## Context

SIA must not claim low observer effect from an unexecuted schema, an unmatched development host, synthetic-only evidence, incomplete endpoint coverage, or an uncalibrated estimate. This ADR preregisters the measurement and decision procedure before benchmark execution. The external benchmark harness, rather than SIA, is authoritative for observer-effect claims.

## Decision

### Effect and unit of analysis

The unit of analysis is one independent matched pair containing an observer-disabled trial and an observer-enabled trial. Both trials use the same workload input, seed, concurrency, duration, host state, and measurement boundary. The observation mechanism under evaluation is the only intentional difference.

For ratio endpoints, pair `i` has effect `(enabled_i / disabled_i) - 1`. For absolute endpoints, it has effect `enabled_i - disabled_i`. Positive and negative effects receive equal treatment. Ratio endpoints require a finite, strictly positive disabled value; otherwise the affected pair is invalid and accounted for as excluded.

The required workloads are:

1. `cpu_batch`: a single-process, single-thread deterministic SHA-256 stream over 64 GiB generated from seed 104729, timed from the first generated byte through verification of the final digest.
2. `gpu_compute`: 300 seconds of repeated 4096 by 4096 FP32 matrix multiplication from seed 130363 at concurrency one, including device synchronization after the final operation.
3. `gui_active`: 300 seconds of the fixed SIA GUI trace identified in the baseline, replayed at 1920 by 1080 and 60 Hz with one active GUI instance and seed 155921.

The immutable executable, input, trace, and expected-output digests must be entered in the baseline before data collection. A missing digest prohibits execution under this contract. Development-host dry runs may validate the harness but are not evidence for a target-hardware claim.

Required endpoint combinations and symmetric equivalence margins are:

| Workload | Endpoint | Effect | Margin | Practical rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| all | target elapsed or frame time | ratio | -0.02 to +0.02 | A two-percent change is the largest operationally negligible target slowdown or speedup. |
| all | SIA CPU core fraction | absolute | -0.005 to +0.005 | Half of one fully utilized core percentage point is the CPU budget. |
| all | SIA peak RSS | absolute bytes | -67108864 to +67108864 | 64 MiB is the memory budget. |
| all | SIA read rate | absolute bytes/second | -1048576 to +1048576 | 1 MiB/s is the read-I/O budget. |
| all | SIA write rate | absolute bytes/second | -1048576 to +1048576 | 1 MiB/s is the write-I/O budget. |
| `gpu_compute`, `gui_active` | target GPU clock | ratio | -0.02 to +0.02 | A two-percent clock displacement can affect accelerator behavior. |
| `gpu_compute`, `gui_active` | target GPU power | ratio | -0.03 to +0.03 | Three percent is the smallest operationally material power displacement. |
| `gpu_compute`, `gui_active` | target GPU temperature | absolute degrees Celsius | -2 to +2 | Two degrees is the thermal-equivalence budget. |

The target endpoint is `target_elapsed_time_ns` for `cpu_batch` and `gpu_compute`, and `target_frame_time_p99_ms` for `gui_active`. The baseline therefore contains 21 required workload-endpoint conclusions. Elapsed time spans all target setup, work, synchronization, validation, and teardown inside the declared boundary. GUI frame-time samples retain their trial distribution; p99 is computed by the nearest-rank rule. CPU time is user plus system CPU time divided by externally measured trial duration. RSS is the trial maximum. I/O values are process read and write bytes divided by trial duration. GPU clock, power, and temperature are time-weighted means over valid external samples. Units and aggregation rules cannot be changed after measurement begins.

### Equivalence and uncertainty

For each endpoint, the estimate is the arithmetic mean of paired effects. A two-sided bias-corrected and accelerated paired bootstrap interval is computed by resampling whole matched pairs with replacement. Exactly 100,000 resamples and seed 8675309 are used. Pairs, never individual arms or samples within a trial, are the resampling unit. Independent pairs and exchangeability of pair effects are assumed; material sequence, period, or carryover detected by the checks below invalidates that workload rather than being ignored.

There are `K = 21` required conclusions. Bonferroni simultaneous coverage is used: each interval has confidence `1 - 0.05 / 21`, or 99.7619047619 percent, giving at least 95 percent family-wise coverage without relying on endpoint independence.

An endpoint passes only when its complete adjusted confidence interval is strictly inside both sides of its preregistered margin. Touching or crossing a margin is not a pass. Margin overlap, failure to reject a difference test, and a favorable point estimate are insufficient. A workload passes only if all its required endpoints pass. The overall conclusion passes only if all 21 endpoints pass. Any failed, missing, invalid, or inconclusive endpoint prevents an overall pass, and conflicting outcomes must be reported rather than selectively summarized.

### Pairing, order, and environmental controls

Pair order is generated before execution with PCG64 seed 424242. Consecutive pairs form blocks of two: one pair is disabled-then-enabled and the other enabled-then-disabled; a seeded coin flip selects the order within each block. An odd final pair uses the less frequent sequence. Pair identifiers and planned order are immutable before outcomes are visible.

Each arm receives one unmeasured warm-up using its assigned mode. A five-minute idle stabilization precedes every measured arm. GPU workloads additionally require GPU temperature within 2 degrees Celsius of that pair's first-arm precondition and within 2 degrees of the session median. Stabilization may continue for at most 15 minutes; failure invalidates the pair.

The host is isolated from interactive use, networking not required by the workload is disabled, automatic updates and scheduled jobs are disabled, and CPU affinity, memory policy, governor, power limit, frequency settings, display configuration, driver, runtime, OS, kernel, and firmware remain fixed. Background non-harness CPU utilization above 2 percent for more than five seconds, non-workload disk throughput above 10 MiB/s for more than five seconds, thermal-throttle flags, power-limit changes, device reset, or clock-policy changes invalidate the pair.

Before inference, each endpoint is regressed on sequence, period, and their interaction using pair-level effects. An adjusted two-sided p-value below 0.01 for sequence or period, or an absolute fitted term above half that endpoint's equivalence margin, is treated as material order or period evidence and makes that workload inconclusive. Cache state is reset using the workload-specific procedure in the baseline before each arm. A reset failure invalidates the pair. Carryover is detected by failed precondition matching, residual device work, changed cache-reset evidence, or a material sequence interaction; it causes invalidation rather than post-hoc correction.

### Repetition and stopping

A fixed pilot of 12 independent pairs per workload estimates the standard deviation `s` of every paired effect. Pilot observations never enter the final analysis. For each endpoint, required final pairs are

`ceil((z * s / (margin_half_width / 2))^2)`,

where `z` is the standard-normal quantile for the 99.7619047619-percent two-sided interval. The workload sample size is the maximum endpoint requirement, at least 30 and at most 200 independent included pairs. The numerical precision target is an adjusted 99.7619047619-percent interval half-width no greater than half the equivalence margin. If the formula requires more than 200 pairs, the workload is inconclusive. The computed count and pilot variances are committed before final collection.

There is no outcome-dependent early stopping and final results are inspected only after the fixed count is attempted. Replacement is allowed only for an objectively invalid pair and remains subject to the 200-attempt maximum. If exclusions leave fewer than the committed included-pair count, the affected workload is inconclusive. This fixed design prevents optional-stopping inflation.

### Failures and exclusions

Every attempted arm and pair is recorded. Timeout, nonzero exit, output mismatch, device reset, missing required sample, invalid denominator, failed synchronization, failed cache reset, environmental-threshold breach, or predeclared throttle condition invalidates the entire pair. A timed-out value is not imputed or silently censored. No outlier rule based on measured arm values is permitted. Anomalous but valid values remain included. Exclusion decisions use event and quality fields without comparing enabled and disabled endpoint outcomes, and each decision records a reason code. Missing arms remain visible in the raw table. Exclusion counts and reasons are reported for every conclusion.

### Measurement quality

Every trial records expected and received sample counts, sample-loss count and rate, collector-lateness maximum and p99, and the configured interval. Sample loss above 0.1 percent or collector-lateness p99 above 10 percent of the sampling interval invalidates the pair. Every `gui_active` trial records SIA GPU-active time and GPU-active fraction from an external collector. Missing quality fields make every affected workload endpoint inconclusive and prevent an overall pass.

Each trial also records the complete target timing or throughput distribution or a content-addressed, losslessly reproducible reference; SIA CPU time, peak RSS, read and write bytes; and, where applicable, target GPU clocks, power, temperature, throttle state, and SIA GPU activity.

### External harness boundary

The external harness timestamps both arms, accounts separately for its own timing and sampling cost, and uses the same collectors in both arms. Harness calibration is repeated for each session and recorded. Calibration overhead above 10 percent of the smallest applicable equivalence margin makes the session invalid.

The harness waits for accelerator completion before its end timestamp. Enabled timing includes SIA startup after the start boundary, buffering, flush, export, synchronization, and observer teardown. Deferred observer work cannot be moved outside the enabled boundary. Disabled timing executes the same lifecycle with only the observation mechanism disabled. Collector clocks are synchronized to one monotonic clock and their offset is recorded.

### Provenance and evidence

Before execution the baseline records immutable source and ADR revisions, complete SIA and harness configuration, observer mode, host identity, OS, kernel, CPU, memory, accelerator, driver and runtime versions, firmware, power and frequency settings, environmental controls, workload/input identity, measurement-tool versions, clock source, and calibration results.

Raw observations must be arm-labeled and pair-addressable and include workload, endpoint, pair, order, timestamps, inclusion status and reason, value, unit, sample index or distribution reference, and quality evidence. Summaries alone are insufficient.

The baseline has deterministic result records for every required combination and for the overall conclusion. An independent implementation must be able to reproduce estimates, intervals, exclusions, and statuses without judgment. Until the proposed contract is approved, all immutable provenance is filled, the declared target is tested, and every gate passes, the only permitted overall status is `inconclusive` and no low-observer-effect claim is permitted.
